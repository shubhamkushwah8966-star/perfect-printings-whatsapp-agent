# Privacy Policy — Perfect Printings WhatsApp Assistant

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Perfect Printings uses a WhatsApp assistant to respond to customer enquiries about printing services, support and quotations.

## Information we receive
When you message us, we may receive your WhatsApp phone number, name, message content and any details you choose to share about your enquiry.

## How we use information
We use this information only to reply to your enquiry, provide customer support, prepare quotations, and improve our service. We do not sell personal information.

## Service providers
Messages may be processed through WhatsApp/Meta, OpenAI and our secure hosting provider solely to operate the assistant and answer enquiries.

## Data retention and security
We take reasonable steps to protect information. Please do not send passwords, banking information or other highly sensitive information through WhatsApp.

For privacy questions or to request help with your data, contact Perfect Printings on WhatsApp: +91 89660 66621.
